Controls
Move and assemble
Drag equipment out of the drawer. Position the burette near the stand clamp and press Space to attach it. Fit the funnel to its mouth in the same way.
Prepare
Put the pipette tip in the sample and press Space to fill. Move over the conical flask and deliver. Position the titrant bottle beside the assembled burette and use Fill via funnel. Remove the funnel before titrating.
Read and deliver
Prime the tip into waste, then record an initial reading. Each press delivers once: 1 mL, 0.1 mL or one 0.05 mL drop. Extra additions can overshoot; waiting cannot.
Observe and mix
Choose any indicator dropper from Equipment and add it above the flask. No indicator is selected for you. Select the flask to swirl. Put the probe tip in liquid to read pH. Readings require mixing.
Repeat
Record the final reading as a trial. Empty the flask into waste, rinse it and take a fresh sample aliquot for another trial. Restart resets all equipment and trials.
