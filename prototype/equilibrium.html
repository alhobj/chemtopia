Practical guide

Use Equipment to retrieve stored apparatus. Choose a source, receiver and portion, then Transfer. These are assisted metered portions, not precise measurements made with a beaker. For a first mixture use 5 mL of each 0.00200 M stock and 10 mL matched solvent. Stocks and references are finite.

Retrieve a cuvette, transfer 3 mL solvent into it, select it and wipe the faces. Move it to the sample well using the action button, close the meter lid and blank. Remove with the lid open. Drain to waste, rinse and refill for each sample: a 0.05 mL film remains.

Record solvent (c=0) and both supplied references, using their certificates. Each reference is an equilibrium mixture in excess iron; its complex concentration is solved, not assumed equal to total SCN. Any dilution/contamination invalidates its original certificate. Then measure your own mixture. Use matched filter/optical conditions.

Make additions of iron, thiocyanate or matrix-matched solvent to a mixture and observe its new concentration and colour. Reset starts fresh apparatus using the same adventure reservation.
