Conductivity

Compare ionic and molecular samples. Build the probe circuit, control spacing and depth, and record conductance.

Controls
Connect
Drag the red and black lead ends onto different probe terminals. Alternatively select each probe and use its lead button. Switch the meter on.
Contact
Drag a probe beside a sample and press Space to clip it to the rim. Both tips must contact the same liquid. Dry NaCl can also be tested with both probes clipped to it.
Compare
Record readings using the same probe geometry. Unclip and move probes to another sample. A small liquid film carries material between samples.
Rinse
Unclip a probe, hold it above the waste beaker and bring the water beaker nearby. Select Rinse probe. The film and rinse water go to waste.
Mix
Pour finite portions between vessels. Salt dissolves rapidly when water is added in this model. Labels describe the supplied contents; mixing changes them.
