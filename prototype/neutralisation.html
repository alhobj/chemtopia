Controls
Move
Drag apparatus from Equipment and around the bench. Objects have solid contacts and fall when unsupported.
Pour
Place a vessel above an open receiver. Space adds one 10 mL portion; each press stops. The 1 mL button permits smaller additions.
Stir
Place the rod beside or in a vessel and press Space. Mixing speeds the thermometer response.
Read
Immerse the thermometer tip. Record initial and final readings with its buttons. They record the displayed value, not an automatically corrected peak.
Insulate
In the cup lab, place the lid near its mouth and press Space to fit it. Remove it before pouring; liquid poured onto a fitted lid spills.
Burn and weigh
In combustion, weigh the burner before and after burning. Place the can on its support and the burner beneath it. Space lights/extinguishes the selected burner. Stop heating before the final weighing.
