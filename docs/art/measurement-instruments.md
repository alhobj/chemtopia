# Measurement instrument artwork

The conductivity and cell meters, colorimeter, cuvettes, probes, metal strips and gel salt bridge are original functional SVG drawings authored in this project with Codex. They reuse the existing B palette, bevels and pixel-like details. No new third-party image or font is included. Seven-segment readings are generated from live measurements and fitted into the instrument faces; they are not text boxes covering an illustration.

Beakers still use the owner's detailed B sprite adaptation. Its original source/provenance remains in [the atlas README](../../prototype/assets/design-b/README.md). The original concept sheets are unchanged. The new instruments are not presented as selected/generated B concept-sheet equipment when no corresponding sprite exists.

Model geometry determines vessel mouths, probe tips, wire terminals, cuvette seating and bridge ends. Contents are generated from current material pools inside glass interiors. The bridge, clips and instrument sample well deliberately contain fitted parts instead of simulating overlapping rigid bodies. Standalone apparatus retains gravity, friction and collisions.

The [walkthrough previews](../design/measurement-labs-prototype-review.md) are unedited Chromium captures of actual operation. The new instruments' detail and readability still need owner review before production artwork is commissioned or generated. Controls/model anchors should be settled before increasing drawing complexity.
