## Cross-cutting skills to check when proposing an activity

The skills section combines practical work, technology, mathematics and inquiry. In original summary, it covers planning a question and method, relevant safety/environmental considerations, measuring variables, collecting and processing observations/data, using representations and uncertainty, drawing conclusions and evaluating weaknesses. See printed pp. 27–32 / PDF pp. 33–38 for precise scope and any AHL-specific detail.

A simulated activity can support these objectives, but should not be described as replacing real practical experience or satisfying the school's experimental programme by itself.

## First topic: mixture separation

Start with **Structure 1.1.1** (printed p. 33 / PDF p. 39), **Tool 1** (printed p. 29 / PDF p. 35), and **Structure 2.2.10** (printed p. 41 / PDF p. 47). See the [specific mapping](separation-techniques.md).

Later suggestions can connect a separation activity to intermolecular interactions and solubility (Structure 2.2), experimental data and purity (Tool 1 / inquiry skills), or a distinct organic practical context involving distillation/reflux (Reactivity 3.2.9, printed p. 60 / PDF p. 66). These are candidate connections, not approval of extra chemistry, a particular solvent or new game mechanics.
